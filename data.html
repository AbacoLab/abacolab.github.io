Four venues, one 5-minute grid

Everything Phisys tests on, since 2017 and back to 2003 for the cash markets, kept current every week and checked the way our own research needs it. Not sold on its own: it is what your tests run on.

Why 5 minutes

Nobody should trade below 15 minutes: we measured it, the typical 5-minute bar does not pay its own round trip. But a test at 1 hour that only knows the hourly bar cannot say whether the stop or the target was hit first, and that is the whole outcome of a trade.

So every series lives on one 5-minute grid, and every test at 15 minutes and above is resolved on the 5-minute path: the stop, the partial targets, the breakeven, the trailing, in the order they actually happened. The higher timeframes are built from the same bars, so nothing disagrees with itself.

How it is checked

  • On the grid. Every timestamp is a multiple of five minutes; a snapshot is never passed off as a closed bar.
  • Holes are holes. A missing bar is missing, never filled with the last value; the known gaps are listed.
  • Zero is not missing. A zero volume is a bar nobody traded; an absent value is absent.
  • Each series against a witness. Prices against a second venue; open interest against the archive; spreads against the order book.
  • Nothing from the future. Every value is stamped at the bar where it was known.

The checks are the same ones our own research runs on. The data is a by-product of using it.
