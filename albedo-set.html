Drawdown

Why walk-forward drawdowns are often smaller

A Fixed setup keeps the same values on every market and in every year.

The walk-forward in the scripts looks at more than profit. Window after window, it scores every candidate setup on profit against drawdown and on how straight its equity line is, using only the bars before. Each score is averaged with the neighbouring setups, so the choice lands in a stable area of good values, never on a lone peak.

It is not free: on some markets the Fixed setup simply earns more. What the walk-forward buys first is a smoother ride.

Of all the coins read for each strategy, those where the walk-forward drawdown was smaller than the Fixed setup's, over the same stretch.

Why it matters

Drawdown is what you live through. The deeper the drop, the more you need just to get back, and the more likely you are to switch a strategy off at its worst moment, which is when most traders do.

A shallower drawdown gives you room: for the same pain you can risk a little more per trade, or sleep better at the same risk.
