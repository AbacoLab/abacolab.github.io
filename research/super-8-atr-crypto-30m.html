88 crypto perpetuals at 30m, each tested on its own, after costs.

How it was tested

The strategy
Super 8, an open-source trend strategy from TradingView, rebuilt rule by rule in our engine.Here every distance is measured in the asset's own volatility (its ATR) instead of a fixed share of the price, so the same rules fit a calm coin and a nervous one.How Super 8 works is on its own page.
The test
Walk-forward by half-years: January to June, July to December.Before each half-year the settings are chosen on all the history before it, then traded on a half-year nobody looked at.Many settings are tried each time; the pick follows a rule fixed before the run.The half-years stitched together make the curve.
Costs
The exchange's fees and the funding each asset really paid, bar by bar.Position size capped at the leverage the exchange allows on that asset.
Assets
Every crypto perpetual listed on Hyperliquid with at least a year of history: 88, none picked by hand.Each one on its own: nothing averaged across assets.
The yardstick
The strategy as written, with its starting values and never tuned, on the same half-years.
