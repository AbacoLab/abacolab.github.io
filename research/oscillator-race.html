The oscillator race: the results

Oscillators and divergences traded the same way: who comes out ahead, asset by asset.

In shortTwo races: 18 oscillators and 17 divergences, each traded the same way, on five assets at 15m, 1H and 4H, each asset on its own.Oscillators: ahead most often uranus (5), cmf (3), vwmacd_hist (3), out of 15 cases (asset by timeframe).Divergences: ahead most often pd_wunder_rsi (3), pd_petero_multi (2), single_div (2), out of 15 cases.Gross, before costs and funding, over the whole history: it says who did best, not who will.
Read with care
the score
A place in the ranking within the race, not a return.A high score means better than the other competitors on that asset and timeframe, not necessarily good: the «result» column says whether it made money.
gross
No costs and no funding.With thousands of cycles at 15m costs would weigh a lot: they are not measured here.
different grids
The competitors do not have grids of the same size: more cells, more chances of finding a peak.
