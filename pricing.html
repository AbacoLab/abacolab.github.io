questions

Questions

Can I try Phisys before paying?

Phisys opens to subscribers only. Cancel in the first 14 days and you get the money back.

Which strategies do I get?

The pack of your plan: Albedo, then Citrinitas, then Rubedo. Each adds strategies and a richer Book. Phisys is built for you to make your own.

What does Telegram tell me?

When something needs you: the machine is down, a venue went quiet, an order was refused, your licence is about to end. Your own price alerts, unlimited in every paid plan. And what your Book did, without the details: those stay on your page.

Does the executor cost extra?

No. It runs your Book in every paid plan. On Hyperliquid each order, in and out, carries Abaco’s builder fee, approved once from your wallet and revocable at any time. Your tests count it among the costs.

Where do my keys go?

Only to your machine, whichever of the two you choose.

Can the executor trade somewhere other than Hyperliquid?

In Magister, on request: Binance or Bybit outside the EU, Kraken or OKX inside it, free with an account opened through our referral link. With an account you already have, it is €55 a month more. Ask before you subscribe.

Do I get anything for opening my exchange account with Abaco?

Yes. Open it through our referral link: the exchange’s own discount on trading fees, and your first month free.

What happens when I stop paying?

Phisys stops at the end of the period you paid. Your strategies stay yours, as files.

Is this investment advice?

No. Phisys is software: it tests the rules you give it and runs them on your machine. We publish measurements; we do not give signals or manage money.
