Risk disclosure

1. Trading can lose everything

Trading, and above all trading with leverage, can lose more than you expect, up to the whole capital in the account. Prices can gap through a stop. Only trade money you can afford to lose.

2. Tests are not results

What Phisys measures, in sample or out of sample, is how a rule behaved on past data. It is hypothetical: it is built with hindsight, it carries no real risk, and it cannot reproduce what a real loss does to the person who suffers it. Costs, slippage and funding can differ from those assumed in a test, orders may not fill at the modelled price, and data may differ from what was available at the time. Treat a test as an upper bound, not an expectation. Past results do not promise future results.

3. Losses are normal

Every rule has losing trades, weeks and years while it behaves as designed. A future loss can be deeper than any seen in a test.

4. Leverage and liquidation

Leverage multiplies losses as well as gains. The exchange can liquidate a position, change its margin or stop quoting at any time, and you have no right to an extension.

5. Automation can fail

Exchanges, networks, Cloudflare and your connection can fail, delay or reject orders. We do not guarantee that an order is filled, at what price, or that the executor and the keeper are always reachable.

6. Decisions are yours

Every rule, setting, position and order is decided by you. The assistant's drafts can be wrong: test them before you use them.
